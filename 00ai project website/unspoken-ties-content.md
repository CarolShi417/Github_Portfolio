## narrative-01

id: narrative-01

type: right-column-text

**narrative-01-p1**:

As Project Manager, I found that our first plan was too ambitious. The team was still learning Unreal Engine and SVN, so progress was slower than expected. I adjusted the timeline, reduced the project scope, and assigned tasks based on each member鈥檚 strengths and interests. This made the workflow smoother and helped us complete a playable demo.

**narrative-01-p2**:

As Level Designer, I turned the father-son story into levels and cooperative puzzles. The story and level design developed together, rather than in separate stages. I also used reference images, notes, and clear scale guidelines when handing work to programmers and artists. Regular communication helped the team understand my ideas and reduced unnecessary revisions.
