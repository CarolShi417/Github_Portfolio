Cast Calm

Calm Cast is a game that combines fishing with the BCI system. The players’ goal in the game is to catch the highest-quality fish possible within the time limit.

The game intends to challenge players’ ability to maintain emotional stability under the game’s increasing pressure. To catch the fish, players need to use the rod sensor to keep the cursor within a certain zone. During this process, the BCI system will constantly monitor players’ emotional state, which directly affects the sensitivity of the rod sensor.

As Project Manager, I found that our first plan was too ambitious. The team was still learning Unreal Engine and SVN, so progress was slower than expected. I adjusted the timeline, reduced the project scope, and assigned tasks based on each member’s strengths and interests. This made the workflow smoother and helped us complete a playable demo.

As Level Designer, I turned the father-son story into levels and cooperative puzzles. The story and level design developed together, rather than in separate stages. I also used reference images, notes, and clear scale guidelines when handing work to programmers and artists. Regular communication helped the team understand my ideas and reduced unnecessary revisions.
