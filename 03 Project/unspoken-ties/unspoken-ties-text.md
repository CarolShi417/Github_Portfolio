## narrative-02

id: narrative-02

type: right-column-text

**narrative-02-p1**:

The demo focuses on **Tutorial Level and Level 1**, designed as a gradually ascending environment that combines exploration, platforming, puzzle-solving, and cooperation. I first planned the level through simple layouts, defining player routes, key interaction points, elevation changes, and collectible locations.

The tutorial uses a linear structure with **a gradual difficulty curve**, introducing movement, Jump + Dash, and each character’s unique abilities step by step. The following section expands into a large central cave surrounded by spiral platforms, where the Father supports the Son’s exploration from outside.

Through playertest feedback layout and blockout iterations, I refined the level to improve navigation, pacing, spatial readability, and the integration of **cooperative mechanics into the environment.**
